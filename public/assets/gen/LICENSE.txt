Own procedural Blender work:
- bldg_1.glb through bldg_8.glb: commercial buildings; tools/blender/building.py.
- photo_a1.glb through photo_a14.glb and photo_t1.glb through photo_t6.glb:
  facade slabs; tools/blender/photo_building.py. Generated elevations and roof
  images are bound from ../photo/; ambientCG cladding is documented in ../tex/.
- tree_big.webp, tree_small.webp: tree billboards rendered with
  tools/blender/tree_billboard.py.

Poly Haven-derived assemblies (not wholly original meshes):
- euro_1.glb through euro_6.glb: assembled by Late Roadster with
  tools/blender/euro_building.py from Poly Haven's modular_urban_apartments_facade.
  Original kit: https://polyhaven.com/a/modular_urban_apartments_facade
  Original license: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Changes: kit parts arranged/scaled into apartment blocks and exported to GLB;
  facade texture/material binding for the game. See ../ph/LICENSE.txt for maps.

Export processing: tools/optimize_glb.mjs and tools/optimize_assets.sh apply
meshopt compression and texture resizing/WebP optimisation where applicable.
Runtime model normalization, material binding/tweaks and selected mesh omission
are performed in src/render/assets.ts using src/render/manifest.ts metadata.
CC0 attribution is supplied for provenance, not because CC0 requires it.
